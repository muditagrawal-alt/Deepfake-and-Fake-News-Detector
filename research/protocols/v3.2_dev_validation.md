# Protocol: adopting engine v3.2 (written 2026-09-29, before any v3.2 development run)

## Question
Does v3.2 (web image search with dHash distances as external verification for media; a
`misinformation_type` output) improve on v3.1.1, the frozen engine?

## Development items only
| Set | Items | Modality |
|---|---|---|
| `mmsoc_politifact__dev30` | 30 FakeNewsNet-PolitiFact training articles (MM-Soc release), stratified | article + image |
| `mmsoc_gossipcop__dev30` | 30 FakeNewsNet-GossipCop training articles (MM-Soc release), stratified | article + image |
| `liar__dev30` | 30 LIAR validation statements, stratified | statement |
| `ffpp_c23__dev20` | 10 original + 10 manipulated FaceForensics++ c23 videos from the official validation split | video |

*Revised 2026-09-29, second time:* the author restricted the benchmark programme to the
benchmarks the reviewers name (FakeNewsNet, LIAR, FaceForensics++) plus the paper's own sets.
The development sets were changed to development splits of those benchmarks. The first
version's runs (MMFakeBench val100, stopped after 5-6 items per engine version) were
discarded without analysis and moved to `runs/out_of_scope/`. The first revision (val200 →
val100, dev50 → dev30) was also made before any result, for compute.

No test item is used. The in-house sets are not used for this decision (I-6 was found on them).

## Systems
v3.1.2 (`main`; v3.1.1 plus search-failure handling) and v3.2 (`engine-dev`, which includes the same fix), same backend and model
(the backend the test runs will use: Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, pinned, if a paid key is added;
otherwise gemma4:12b via Ollama), same settings, one run each. Fact-check sites are excluded
for the news sets, as in their test runs.

## Decision rule
Paired accuracy on the pooled 110 development items, exact McNemar test.
- **Adopt v3.2** if its pooled accuracy is higher and it is not significantly worse
  (McNemar p < 0.05 against it) on any of the four sets.
- Otherwise keep v3.1.1 for the benchmark programme and report v3.2 as a negative result.

Secondary, reported but not deciding: per-set accuracy, macro-F1, AUC; latency, tokens and
cost per item.

## After the decision
The adopted version is frozen. Test runs are made once per benchmark with it. If v3.2 is
adopted, the in-house runs are repeated with it and reported as post-hoc next to v3.1 (I-6).
