# Protocol: adopting engine v3.2 (written 2026-09-29, before any v3.2 development run)

## Question
Does v3.2 (web image search with dHash distances as external verification for media; a
`misinformation_type` output) improve on v3.1.1, the frozen engine?

## Development items only
| Set | Items | Modality | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mmfakebench__val200` | 200 of MMFakeBench's validation pairs, stratified by type | image + caption | covers false text, generated/edited images and image–text mismatch |
| `celebdf_v2__dev20` | 20 Celeb-DF videos outside the official test list | video | face swaps of real people |
| `mmsoc_politifact__dev50` | 50 MM-Soc PolitiFact training articles | article + image | news with images |

No test item is used. The in-house sets are not used for this decision (I-6 was found on them).

## Systems
v3.1.1 (`main`, engine tree `4692dce`) and v3.2 (`engine-dev`), same backend and model
(gemma4:12b via Ollama, v3.1.1 repair included in both), same settings, one run each.

## Decision rule
Paired accuracy on the pooled 270 development items, exact McNemar test.
- **Adopt v3.2** if its pooled accuracy is higher and it is not significantly worse
  (McNemar p < 0.05 against it) on any of the three sets.
- Otherwise keep v3.1.1 for the benchmark programme and report v3.2 as a negative result.

Secondary, reported but not deciding: per-set accuracy, macro-F1, AUC; on MMFakeBench, the
4-class macro-F1 of v3.2's `misinformation_type` (v3.1.1 has no such output); latency and
tokens per item.

## After the decision
The adopted version is frozen. Test runs are made once per benchmark with it. If v3.2 is
adopted, the in-house runs are repeated with it and reported as post-hoc next to v3.1 (I-6).
